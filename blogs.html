Lets Explore Varkala Together





Varkala is a coastal town in the southern part of Kerala, India. It is known for its scenic beaches, especially Varkala Beach, and cliffs that rise dramatically from the Arabian Sea. The town is also famous for the Papanasam Beach, considered a sacred spot for pilgrims to cleanse themselves of their sins. Janardanaswamy Temple, a 2000-year-old shrine, is another key attraction in Varkala Varkala is known for its backpacker-friendly vibe, with various cafes, hostels, and guesthouses. It's a popular destination for yoga and ayurvedic treatments as well. The town offers a relaxed atmosphere, attracting tourists seeking both adventure and peace. Some other important attractions include the Kappil Lake, Sivagiri Mutt, and Anjengo Fort. Varkala is also well-connected to Trivandrum, which is about 37 km away
